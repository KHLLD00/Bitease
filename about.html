Our Story

Delight, one nibble at a time.

How a bakery counter in Lokoja grew into breakfast, pizza night, and everything in between.

BiteEase started as a simple idea: a corner of Lokoja where you could get something genuinely good to eat at almost any hour — a warm loaf in the morning, a proper meal at lunch, and a slice of pizza worth staying out for at night.

What began with fresh bread and pastries grew, batch by batch, into a full kitchen: shakshuka and waffles for breakfast, wood-fired pizza, shawarma wrapped to order, burgers stacked two layers high, and a drinks counter that goes from smoothies to soft-serve. Every category on our menu started because someone asked for it, and we said yes.

We're still a small, single-location kitchen, opposite the Civil Defense HQ, before the Zone 8 Roundabout — which means everything is made close to where it's eaten, and usually close to when you ordered it.

Delight in every nibble isn't just a tagline — it's the standard we hold every tray, wrap, and cup to before it leaves the kitchen.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick breakfast, ordering a pizza for the whole house, or just want an ice cream cone on your way past Zone 8 — we're glad to have you.
